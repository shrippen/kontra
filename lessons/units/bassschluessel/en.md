# The bass clef
id: bassschluessel
for: any
needs: dur-tonleiter
terms: bassschluessel notenlinien

## Five lines
Notes are written on five [[notenlinien|staff lines]]: on the lines and in the spaces. The higher the note, the higher
it sounds. The [[bassschluessel|bass clef]] at the start sets which line is which note: its two dots surround the F
line, the second from the top.
::: hint
Bass sounds an octave lower than written. That way its notes fit on the staff without many ledger lines.
:::

## The lines
From bottom to top:
- Lines: G, B, D, F, A.
- Spaces: A, C, E, G.
Below them lie F, just under the bottom line, and the low E on a ledger line.

## Practise
C major slowly. Set the view to "Notation" and read along.
::: practice bundled=8 speeds="60 80 100"

## Check

:::
