## The foundation
The [[grundton|root]] gives a chord its name. The [[quinte|fifth]] lies seven semitones above it and sounds almost
like one note with it. Root and fifth in turn: one of the oldest bass lines, from country to rock.
::: listen root=A steps="0 7 0 7"
::: figure root=A steps="0 7" labels="1 5"
::: variant for=strings
On the bass the fifth lies one string higher and two positions further.
:::

## Try it
Play A and its fifth E.
::: try root=A steps="0 7"
