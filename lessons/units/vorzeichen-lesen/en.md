# Reading accidentals
id: vorzeichen-lesen
for: any
needs: rhythmus-lesen tonarten
terms: generalvorzeichen aufloesungszeichen

## At the start of each line
The [[generalvorzeichen|key signature]] comes right after the clef and holds for the whole piece, in every octave. A
sharp on the F line means: every F becomes F♯. It tells you the key.

## In the middle of a bar
An accidental before a single note holds until the end of the bar. The [[aufloesungszeichen|natural sign]] ♮ cancels
an accidental.

## Practise
F major, one flat at the start. View "Notation".
