# Following a chord progression
id: akkorde-folgen
for: any
needs: form akkordnamen

## Roots first
If you only get chord symbols, play only the roots first, one per chord. Once that sits, add rhythm, fifth and octave,
passing notes last.

## Think ahead
On the last beat of a chord, already look at the next one. That way you land on 1 safely.

## Practise
Offbeat groove over I-V-VI-IV in C.
