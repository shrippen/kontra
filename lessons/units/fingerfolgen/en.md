## Every finger works
Finger patterns train all four fingers of the fretting hand, the little one too. The [[fingersatz|fingering]]
1-2-3-4 means: index, middle, ring, little finger, one note further each, the same on every string.
::: figure root=A steps="2 3 4 5" labels="1 2 3 4" string=A

## Slow is fast
Play slowly enough that every note sounds clean and the fingers stay close to the strings. Speed comes by itself;
tension does not go away again.
::: hint
If something hurts, take a break. Staying loose matters more than pushing through.
:::

## Practise
First 1-2-3-4, then 1-3-2-4.
