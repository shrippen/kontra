# With the drums
id: ruf-antwort
for: any
needs: pocket schlagzeug

## Bass and kick drum
Bass and [[bassdrum|kick drum]] often play the same beats. Listen to the kick and place your notes with it; where the
[[snare|snare]] hits, the bass often leaves room.

## Call and response
Leave gaps. A rest in the bass gives the drums room to answer, and the next bass note lands harder. Fewer notes often
groove more.

## Practise
A groove with rests in A. Listen to kick and bass falling together.
