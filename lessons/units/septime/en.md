## The wide intervals
- The major [[sexte|sixth]] (nine semitones) sounds wide and warm.
- The minor [[septime|seventh]] (ten semitones) sounds like the blues: it wants to move on, back to the root.
- The major seventh (eleven semitones) lies a semitone below the octave and sounds tense.

::: listen root=A steps="0 10"

## Finding the seventh
The minor seventh lies a whole tone below the [[oktave|octave]]. If you know the octave, the seventh is two semitones
below it.
