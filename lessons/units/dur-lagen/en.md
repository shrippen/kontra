## Without moving the hand
In a [[lage|position]] the fretting hand stays in one place, each finger has its fret. G major from the second fret:
index finger fret 2, middle 3, ring 4, little finger 5. The scale runs across three strings without the hand moving:
- E string: G with the middle finger, A with the little finger.
- A string: B, C, D with index, middle and little finger.
- D string: E, F♯, G with index, ring and little finger.
::: figure root=G steps="0 2" labels="1 2" string=E
::: figure root=B steps="0 1 3" labels="3 4 5" string=A
::: figure root=E steps="12 14 15" labels="6 7 8" string=D
::: hint
The same shape works for every major key. Slide it up or down the neck, and the root sets the key.
:::

## Practise
G major in this position, then D major with the same shape: root at the fifth fret of the A string.
::: practice bundled=7 speeds="60 80 100"
