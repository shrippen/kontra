# Notes on the E and A strings
id: toene-e-a
for: strings
needs: halbton

## Finding the notes
Most bass lines start on the E or A string. Knowing the notes there lets you find the [[grundton|root]] of every
chord. On the E string you find:
::: figure root=E steps="0 1 3 5 7 8 10 12" labels="0 1 3 5 7 8 10 12" string=E
On the A string the notes lie differently: A to B is a whole step, E to F only a half step:
::: figure root=A steps="0 2 3 5 7 8 10 12" labels="0 2 3 5 7 8 10 12" string=A

## A memory aid
From 12 on everything repeats an octave higher. If you know G on the E string (3) and C on the A string (3), count on
from there in semitones and whole tones.

## Try it
On the A string, play A, B, C, D.
::: try root=A steps="0 2 3 5" string=A
And on the E string G, A, B.
::: try root=E steps="3 5 7" string=E

## Check
::: question
Which note is at position 5 on the E string?
- [ ] G
- [x] A
- [ ] B
:::
