# The major scale
id: dur-tonleiter
for: any
needs: intervall
terms: tonleiter dur

## Seven notes
A [[tonleiter|scale]] is a row of notes from the root up to its octave. The [[dur|major]] scale has seven notes, then
it starts again. Almost every song that sounds happy uses it.
::: listen root=C steps="0 2 4 5 7 9 11 12"

## The pattern
Whole steps and half steps lie between the notes, always in the same order: whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole,
half. With this pattern you build major from any root.
::: figure root=G steps="0 2 4 5 7 9 11 12" labels="1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8"
::: hint
The half steps lie between 3 and 4 and between 7 and 8. There the scale sounds as if it wants to move on.
:::

## Try it
Play G major from G up to the G an octave higher.
::: try root=G steps="0 2 4 5 7 9 11 12"

## Practise
G major slowly, up and down.
::: practice bundled=7 speeds="70 85 100"
