## How long, without counting
The shape of a note tells its length:
- Whole: open head, no stem.
- Half: open head with a [[notenhals|stem]].
- Quarter: filled head with a stem.
- Eighth: plus a flag; several eighths are joined by a beam.
- Sixteenth: two flags or two beams.
A dot after the note lengthens it by half.

## Clap first
Read a bar, clap it, then play it. Once you know the rhythm, you only have to find the notes while playing.
