# Slap and pop
id: slap-pop
for: e-bass
needs: dead-notes oktave
terms: slap

## Thumb and index finger
For a [[slap|slap]] the side of the thumb strikes the low string at the end of the neck and bounces straight back. For
a pop the index finger hooks under a high string and lets it snap against the frets. They often alternate: low note
with the thumb, the octave with a pop.
::: figure root=E steps="0 12" labels="1 8"
::: hint
The motion comes from the wrist, like knocking on a door. The thumb does not stay on the string.
:::

## Practise
Slap on one chord in E, slowly.
::: practice bundled=134 speeds="50 70 90"

## Check
::: question
What do you play the pop with?
- [ ] the thumb
- [x] the index finger lifting the string
- [ ] the fretting hand
:::
