## Darker
[[moll|Minor]] sounds more serious than major. The minor scale has a minor third, a minor sixth and a minor seventh.
Its pattern: whole, half, whole, whole, half, whole, whole.
::: listen root=A steps="0 2 3 5 7 8 10 12"
::: figure root=A steps="0 2 3 5 7 8 10 12" labels="1 2 b3 4 5 b6 b7 8"

## Related to major
A minor has the same notes as C major; it just starts on A. Every major key has such a minor sibling, three semitones
lower.

## Try it
Play A minor from A to A.
::: try root=A steps="0 2 3 5 7 8 10 12"
