## Chords one note at a time
A bass seldom plays chords all at once. It plays their notes one after another, as an [[arpeggio|arpeggio]]: root,
third, fifth, maybe the octave. That way the harmony sounds and the line keeps moving.
::: listen root=A steps="0 4 7 12 7 4"
::: figure root=A steps="0 4 7 12" labels="1 3 5 8"

## Try it
Play the A major arpeggio up and back down.
::: try root=A steps="0 4 7 12 7 4 0"

## Practise
Triads over I-V-vi-IV in F, each chord as an arpeggio.
::: practice bundled=68 speeds="60 80 100"
