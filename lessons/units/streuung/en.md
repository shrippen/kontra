## How far the notes spread
The [[streuung|spread]] says how widely your notes are scattered around their average placement, in milliseconds. A
small spread means: each note sits where the one before sat. You hear that as a solid groove, even if the placement
itself is not exactly on the beat.
::: hint
The summary shows the spread as a number and as the "Timing" chart: the narrower the hill, the more even.
:::

## Practise
Eighths over I-IV-V in E; the tempo rises from 80 to 110 BPM. Keep the spread small as it gets faster.
::: practice bundled=78 speeds="70 85 100"

## Check
::: question

- [ ] that you play exactly on the beat
- [x] that your notes sit evenly
- [ ] that you play quietly
:::
