# Twelve notes, semitone and whole tone
id: halbton
for: any
needs: viertel
terms: halbton ganzton vorzeichen

## Twelve notes
The music you play knows twelve notes, then everything starts again an octave higher. The smallest step between two
notes is a [[halbton|semitone]]; two semitones are a [[ganzton|whole tone]].
::: listen root=E steps="0 1 2"
::: variant for=fretted
On the bass a semitone is one fret further, a whole tone two frets.
:::

## Sharp and flat
Seven notes have their own names: C D E F G A B. The notes in between get an [[vorzeichen|accidental]]: ♯ (sharp)
raises a note by a semitone, ♭ (flat) lowers it by a semitone. Between E and F and between B and C there is no note.

## Try it
Play three notes, each a semitone higher: E, F, F♯.
::: try root=E steps="0 1 2" string=E
