## Major and minor
The [[terz|third]] is the interval that most shapes a chord's character. The major third (four semitones) sounds
bright, major; the minor third (three semitones) darker, minor.
::: listen root=C steps="0 4"
::: listen root=C steps="0 3"
::: figure root=A steps="0 3 4" labels="1 b3 3"

## Try it
Play A and its major third C♯.
::: try root=A steps="0 4"
Now A and the minor third C.
::: try root=A steps="0 3"
