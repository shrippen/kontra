# Changing strings
id: saitenwechsel
for: e-bass
needs: anschlag
terms: saitenwechsel

## The finger moves along
When [[saitenwechsel|changing strings]], index and middle keep alternating even when the string changes. The thumb
moves along: when you play the A string, it rests on the E string and mutes it.
- From low to high: after the stroke the finger lands on the next lower string, as usual.
- From high to low: reach back briefly, keep the movement small.

## Try it
Play the four open strings from low to high: E, A, D, G.
::: try root=E steps="0 5 10 15"
::: hint
Slowly and evenly. If the previous string keeps ringing, the next lesson helps.
:::

## Practise
Root and fifth over a 12-bar blues in A: two strings in turn.
::: practice bundled=64 speeds="60 80 100"

## Check
::: question
What does the thumb do while you play the A string?
- [ ] It stays on the pickup.
- [x] It rests on the E string and mutes it.
- [ ] It plucks along.
:::
