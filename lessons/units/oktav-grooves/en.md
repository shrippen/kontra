## One shape for everything
The [[oktavgriff|octave shape]]: index finger on the root, little finger two strings higher and two places further.
The shape stays the same whatever the note. Disco, funk and rock build whole choruses on it.
::: figure root=A steps="0 12" labels="1 8"
::: hint
Mute the string in between with the underside of the index finger, or it rings along.
:::

## Practise
Octaves over a minor cadence in D minor.
::: practice bundled=42 speeds="60 80 100"
