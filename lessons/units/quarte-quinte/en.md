## Two strong intervals
The [[quarte|fourth]] lies five semitones above the root, the [[quinte|fifth]] seven. A fourth up gives the same note
name as a fifth down: A and D, once D above A, once D below A.
::: listen root=E steps="0 5"
::: listen root=E steps="0 7"
::: variant for=strings
On a bass tuned in fourths the fourth lies on the next string at the same position.
:::

## Try it
Play E and its fourth A.
::: try root=E steps="0 5"
Now E and the fifth B.
::: try root=E steps="0 7"
