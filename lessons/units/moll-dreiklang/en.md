## Root, minor third, fifth
The minor triad differs from the major triad in one note: the third lies a semitone lower.
::: listen root=A steps="0 4 7"
::: listen root=A steps="0 3 7"
::: figure root=A steps="0 3 7 12" labels="1 b3 5 8"

## Try it
Play A minor as an arpeggio: A, C, E and the high A.
::: try root=A steps="0 3 7 12"

## Practise
