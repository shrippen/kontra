## One and two and
Every beat is a [[zaehlzeit|count]]: 1 2 3 4. In between lies the "and": 1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and. So every half beat has
a name, and you always know where you are in the bar.
::: listen root=A steps="0 0 0 0 0 0 0 0"

## Count out loud
Count along while practising, out loud or quietly, even if it feels odd at first. If you count, a mistake does not
throw you out of time: you come back in on the next one.
::: hint
Mistakes are normal. What matters is not to stop but to keep playing on the next beat.
:::

## Practise
Eighth notes on the open A string: a note on every number and every "and". Only when you pluck counts here.
::: practice bundled=52 speeds="70 85 100"

## Check
::: question
How do you count four beats with their half beats?
- [x] 1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and
- [ ] 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8
- [ ] 1 e and a
:::
