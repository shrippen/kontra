## Bass and drums
Bass and drums together are the rhythm section. The [[bassdrum|bass drum]] is the low drum, usually on 1 and 3; the
[[snare|snare]] cracks mostly on 2 and 4. The bass often plays together with the bass drum.
::: hint
Listen to the bass drum while you practise: when your note lands with it, the band sounds like one instrument.
:::

## Metronome or drums
The metronome only gives the beats. The drums also show where the bar starts and where the accents are. In Kontra you
can turn both up or down in the mixer.

## Practise
Eighths over I-IV-V in E with drums. Listen whether your notes land with the bass drum.
::: practice bundled=54 speeds="70 85 100"
