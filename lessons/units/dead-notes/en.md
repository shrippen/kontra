## A click instead of a note
For a [[dead-note|dead note]] the fretting hand rests lightly on the string without pressing it down. Plucking gives a
percussive click without pitch. In tab it is written as x.
::: hint
The fretting hand only touches the string. Press too hard and a note sounds after all.
:::

## Practise
Dead notes on one chord in A. Kontra judges the timing here; the click has no pitch.
