## Four per beat
A [[sechzehntel|sixteenth note]] lasts a quarter of a beat. Count "1 e and a, 2 e and a". Funk and fast rock live on
them.

## Stay relaxed
With sixteenths, calm matters, not force. Play quietly and evenly; when it gets tight, take the tempo back.
::: variant for=e-bass
Keep strictly to alternate plucking: index and middle finger always alternating, also across string changes.
:::

## Practise
Sixteenths on one chord in E.
::: practice bundled=41 speeds="50 70 90"
