## Not everything is four
The [[taktart|time signature]] at the start says how many beats a bar has and which note value counts as one beat:
- 4/4: four quarters, the usual.
- 3/4: three quarters, the waltz. The 1 is accented.
- 6/8: six eighths in two groups of three. It swings in two big beats.

## Practise
First 3/4 on one chord in D, then 6/8 in A.

::: practice bundled=103 speeds="60 80 100"
