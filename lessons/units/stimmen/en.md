## Why tune?
Strings go out of tune with heat, cold and playing. An out-of-tune bass sounds wrong even when you fret everything
right, and Kontra then hears wrong notes. So [[stimmen|tune]] before every practice.
::: variant for=bass
Standard tuning is E A D G; a five-string adds a low B string.
:::

## With the tuner
Pluck an open string and read the tuner.
::: tuner
- Too low: turn the tuner so the string gets tighter.
- Too high: loosen a little first, then come up to the note from below. The tuning holds longer that way.
- One string after the other, then check all of them once more.

## Check
::: question
The A string sounds too low. What do you do?
- [x] Tighten the string until the tuner shows A.
- [ ] Loosen the string.
- [ ] Fret higher up.
:::
