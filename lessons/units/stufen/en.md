## Chords by number
Each note of a scale carries a chord. They are counted as [[stufe|degrees]] in Roman numerals. In A major:
- I: A, the root, home.
- IV: D, away from home.
- V: E, wants to return to I.
::: listen root=A steps="0 5 7 0"

## Why numbers?
With degrees a chord progression works in every key. The blues is I-IV-V over twelve bars; in A that is A, D and E,
in E it is E, A and B.
::: figure root=A steps="0 5 7" labels="I IV V"
