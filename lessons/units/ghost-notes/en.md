## Barely audible
A [[ghost-note|ghost note]] is a note felt more than heard: very quiet, short, between the accented notes. It fills the
groove without crowding it.
::: hint
Play the ghost note at half strength or less. The accented notes before and after stay loud.
:::

## Practise
Ghost notes on one chord in E.
::: practice bundled=44 speeds="60 80 100"

## Check
::: question
How does a ghost note sound?
- [ ] louder than the other notes
- [x] very quiet and short
- [ ] like a chord
:::
