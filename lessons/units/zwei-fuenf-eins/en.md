## The turn of jazz
The [[zwei-fuenf-eins|II-V-I]] leads home in three chords: in C major Dm7, G7, Cmaj7. The roots fall in fifths: D, G,
C. Jazz standards are largely made of it, in changing keys.
::: listen root=C steps="2 7 0"

## Try it
Play the roots of II, V and I in C.
::: try root=C steps="2 7 0" octave=any
