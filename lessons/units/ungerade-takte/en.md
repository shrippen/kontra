# Five and seven

## Odd meters
5/4 and 7/8 sound as if the bar stumbles. They split into small groups that you count one by one:
- 5/4: 3 + 2, "1 2 3, 1 2".
- 7/8: 2 + 2 + 3, "1 2, 1 2, 1 2 3".
The first count of each group is accented.

## Practise
First 5/4 on one chord in E, then 7/8.

::: practice bundled=109 speeds="60 80 100"
