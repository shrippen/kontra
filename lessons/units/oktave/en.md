## The same note, higher
An [[oktave|octave]] is twelve semitones further. Both notes have the same name and sound almost like one. Jumping up
an octave gives a line drive without changing the harmony.
::: listen root=A steps="0 12"
::: figure root=A steps="0 12" labels="1 8"
::: variant for=strings
On the bass the octave lies two strings higher and two positions further towards the body.
:::

## Try it
Play A and the A an octave higher.
::: try root=A steps="0 12"

## Practise
Root and octave on one chord in F.
::: practice bundled=21 speeds="70 85 100"
