# A line of your own
id: eigene-linie
for: any
needs: akkorde-folgen durchgangstoene

## From building blocks
A bass line of your own grows from what you know:
- Root on 1.
- Fifth and octave for movement.
- A passing note before the next chord.
- Rests so the groove can breathe.
Start simple and only add once the simple version grooves.

## Try it
Play a line of your own over the chords. The tab shows an example; Kontra does not judge whether you hit it, but how
your tones fit: chord tones, tones of the key, tones outside. Where the example rests, leave room.
::: practice kind=groove variant=145 rhythm=rests key=A bpm=80 judging=open
