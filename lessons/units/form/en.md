## Parts that come back
The [[form|form]] describes which parts a piece is made of, and in what order:
- Verse: new words, same music.
- Chorus: the part everyone sings along.
- Bridge: something different once, before the chorus returns.
Knowing the form, you know what comes next and do not get lost.

## Practise
A whole pop piece in G with verse, chorus and bridge. Watch for the changes.
