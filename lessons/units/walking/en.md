## One note per beat
A [[walking-bass|walking bass]] moves in quarters, one note on every beat. The rule to start with:
- On 1 the root of the chord.
- On 2 and 3 notes from the chord: third, fifth, seventh.
- On 4 a note that leads to the next root.
::: listen root=C steps="0 4 7 9 2 5 9 7"

## Practise
Walking bass over II-V-I in C.
::: practice bundled=27 speeds="60 80 100"
