# Plucking with two fingers
id: anschlag
for: e-bass
needs: bass-teile
terms: wechselschlag

## Index and middle finger
Your thumb rests on the pickup or on the E string. Pluck through the string with the fingertip until the finger comes
to rest on the next lower string. Index and middle finger take turns: that is
[[wechselschlag|alternate plucking]].
- Do not pull or snap: the finger moves towards the lower string, not away from the bass.
- Both fingers should sound equally loud.

## Try it
Play the open A string four times, alternating index and middle finger.
::: try root=A steps="0 0 0 0" string=A
::: hint
Even is more important than fast.
:::

## Practise
Quarter notes on the open A string with drums. Only the timing of your attack counts here.
::: practice bundled=2 speeds="70 85 100"

## Check
::: question
Where does the finger go after plucking?
- [ ] Away from the bass, upwards.
- [x] It comes to rest on the next lower string.
- [ ] Back to the same string.
:::
