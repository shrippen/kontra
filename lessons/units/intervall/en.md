# What is an interval?
id: intervall
for: any
needs: halbton
terms: intervall

## The distance between two notes
An [[intervall|interval]] is the distance between two notes, counted in semitones. Each interval has a name and a
sound of its own: the [[oktave|octave]] sounds open and calm, the [[quinte|fifth]] strong, the third decides whether
something sounds happy or sad.
::: listen root=A steps="0 12"
::: listen root=A steps="0 7"

## The names
You count from the lower note, which is 1 itself: second (2), third (3), fourth (4), fifth (5), sixth (6), seventh
(7), octave (8). Seconds, thirds, sixths and sevenths come
major and minor, a semitone apart; fourths, fifths and octaves are called perfect.
