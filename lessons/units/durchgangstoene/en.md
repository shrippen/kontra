## A half step before
A [[durchgangston|passing note]] does not belong to the chord but leads to the next note. The half step directly above
or below the next root pulls hardest. Here G♭ leads from C to F:
::: listen root=C steps="0 4 7 6 5"

## Try it
Play C, E, G, then G♭ and land on F.
::: try root=C steps="0 4 7 6 5"

## Practise
Walking bass over I-vi-ii-V in F.
::: practice bundled=81 speeds="60 80 100"

## Check
::: question
Where does a chromatic passing note lie?
- [ ] on 1
- [x] a half step next to the target, usually on 4
- [ ] a fifth above the root
:::
