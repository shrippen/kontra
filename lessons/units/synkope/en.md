## Between the beats
[[synkope|Syncopation]] accents a note that lies between two beats, on the "and". Often it rings across the next beat.
That gives the groove a push forward.
::: hint
Count "1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and" out loud and play only on some "ands". The beat keeps going even when you do not play.
:::

## Tresillo
A well-known pattern: three notes in a bar, on 1, on the "and" of 2 and on 4. It carries Latin, pop and reggaeton.

## Practise
Tresillo over I-V-vi-IV in A. Slowly at first, until the notes between the beats sit securely.
::: practice bundled=72 speeds="60 80 100"
