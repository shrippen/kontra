# Reading chord names
id: akkordnamen
for: any
needs: septakkord
terms: akkordsymbol

## What is written above the notes
A [[akkordsymbol|chord symbol]] names the root and says what comes with it. For the bass the root matters most:
- **A**: A major.
- **Am**: A minor.
- **A7**: A major with a minor seventh.
- **Amaj7**: A major with a major seventh.
- **Am7**: A minor with a minor seventh.
- **A/C♯**: A major, but the bass plays C♯.

## The slash
After a slash comes the bass note. It is meant for you: with **G/B** you play B, not G.
::: hint
German chord symbols write H for B and B for B♭.
:::

## Practise
Arpeggios over I-V-VI-IV in D: D, A, Bm, G.
::: practice bundled=67 speeds="60 80 100"
