## Two notes per beat
An [[achtel|eighth note]] lasts half a beat. Eight of them fill a 4/4 [[takt|bar]]. Eighths drive many rock and pop
songs: even, without gaps.
::: listen root=E steps="0 0 0 0 0 0 0 0"

## Stay even
The eighths on the "and" are just as long and just as loud as those on the number. When it gets faster, the spacing
stays the same; only the tempo goes up.
::: variant for=e-bass
Alternating fingers help: index on the number, middle on the "and", or the other way round.
:::

## Practise
Eighth notes on the low E with drums.
::: practice bundled=9 speeds="70 85 100"
