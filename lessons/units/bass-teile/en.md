# The bass and its parts

for: e-bass
terms: leersaite bund

## What you are holding
An electric bass usually has four **strings**. From the thickest to the thinnest they are E, A, D and G. Pluck a
string without pressing it down and you hear the [[leersaite|open string]].
::: figure root=E steps="0 5 10 15" labels="E A D G"
- The metal strips on the **neck** are the [[bund|frets]]. Press behind a fret and the note goes up.
- On the **headstock** you tune the strings with the tuners.
- In the **body** the pickups take up the sound; the cable goes into the jack.

## Holding it
Sitting, the body rests on your right leg and the neck points slightly up. Standing, the strap holds the bass at the
same height as when sitting. Shoulders loose, wrists as straight as you can.
::: hint
Kontra cannot see how you hold the bass. Check once in a mirror or film yourself with your phone.
:::

## Try it
Pluck the thickest string without pressing it down. That is the low E.
::: try root=E steps="0" string=E
Now the thinnest string, the G.
::: try root=E steps="15" string=G
