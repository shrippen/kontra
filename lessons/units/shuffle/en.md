## Long, short
In a [[shuffle|shuffle]] two eighths are not equally long: the first takes two thirds of the beat, the second one
third. It sounds like a triplet with the middle missing: "long short, long short". Blues and swing move like this.
::: hint
In notation a shuffle is often written as straight eighths with a note at the start. It is still played long, short.
:::
