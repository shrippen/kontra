## One song, one scale
The [[tonart|key]] says which scale a song takes its notes from. A song in G major uses the notes of the G major scale,
and G feels like home.

## Sharps and flats
To keep the pattern whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half, most keys need [[vorzeichen|accidentals]]:
- C major: none. Only the natural notes C D E F G A B.
- G major: one sharp, F♯.
- F major: one flat, B♭.
::: figure root=F steps="0 2 4 5 7 9 11 12" labels="1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8"

## Try it
Play F major. Listen to the fourth note.
::: try root=F steps="0 2 4 5 7 9 11 12"
