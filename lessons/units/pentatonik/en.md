## Five notes that always fit
The [[pentatonik|pentatonic]] is a scale with five notes. It leaves out the half steps, so hardly a note from it
sounds wrong. Rock, blues and funk live on it.
- Minor pentatonic: 1, b3, 4, 5, b7.
- Major pentatonic: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6.
::: listen root=A steps="0 3 5 7 10 12"
::: figure root=A steps="0 3 5 7 10 12" labels="1 b3 4 5 b7 8"

## Try it
Play the A minor pentatonic upwards.
::: try root=A steps="0 3 5 7 10 12"

## Practise
First minor pentatonic in A, then major pentatonic in G.
