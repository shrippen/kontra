# Quarter notes in time
id: viertel
for: any
terms: takt viertel bpm metronom

## Counting
Most songs are in a [[takt|bar]] of four beats: 1 2 3 4, 1 2 3 4. A [[viertel|quarter note]] lasts exactly one beat.
How fast the beats come is the tempo in [[bpm|BPM]]: beats per minute.
::: listen root=E steps="0 0 0 0"

## Count and play
Count out loud and play one note on every number. A [[metronom|metronome]] or the drums give the beats; your note
should land exactly on the beat, not before and not after.
::: hint
In the first exercises only the timing of your attack counts. Which note sounds does not matter.
:::

## Practise
Quarter notes on the low E with drums, slowly at first.
::: practice bundled=1 speeds="70 85 100"

## Check
