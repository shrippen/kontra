## Three notes
A [[akkord|chord]] is notes that sound together. The simplest is the [[dreiklang|triad]]: root, third and fifth. The
third decides between major and minor:
- Major: major third (4 semitones), then fifth (7).
- Minor: minor third (3 semitones), then fifth (7).
::: listen root=C steps="0 4 7"
::: listen root=C steps="0 3 7"
::: figure root=G steps="0 4 7" labels="1 3 5"

## Try it
Play G major as a triad: G, B, D.
::: try root=G steps="0 4 7"
