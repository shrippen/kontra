## One more note
A [[septakkord|seventh chord]] is a triad with a seventh on top. Three kinds come up all the time:
- Major seventh (maj7): major triad and major seventh. Sounds soft.
- Dominant seventh (7): major triad and minor seventh. Sounds like blues and wants to move on.
- Minor seventh (m7): minor triad and minor seventh. Sounds calm, like jazz and soul.
