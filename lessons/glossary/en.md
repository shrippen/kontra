## Open string
id: leersaite
for: strings
lesson: bass-teile
A string you pluck without pressing it down. Tabs write it as 0.

## Fret
id: bund
for: e-bass
lesson: greifen
The metal strips across the neck. From fret to fret the note goes up a semitone.
::: variant for=fretted
You press right behind it.
:::
::: variant for=fretless
Your bass has none; the markers show where they would be, and that is where you press.
:::

## Tuning
id: stimmen
for: strings
lesson: stimmen
Setting the strings so that each open string hits its note exactly. Before every practice, with the tuner.

## Alternate plucking
id: wechselschlag
for: e-bass
lesson: anschlag
Index and middle finger of the plucking hand take turns. The attack stays even, also when it gets faster.

## Bar
id: takt
lesson: viertel
A group of beats, usually four (1 2 3 4). The time signature says how many beats of which length: 4/4 means four
quarter notes.

## Quarter note
id: viertel
lesson: viertel
A note lasting one beat. Four quarter notes fill a 4/4 bar.

## BPM
id: bpm
lesson: viertel
Beats per minute: the tempo. 60 BPM is one beat per second, 120 BPM two.

## Metronome
id: metronom
lesson: viertel
Gives the beats as an even click. Kontra has one in every mode, and drums in the grooves.

## Note value
id: notenwert
lesson: notenwerte
How long a note or rest lasts, in beats: whole (4), half (2), quarter (1), eighth (½).

## Rest
id: pause
lesson: notenwerte
A note value without a note. You keep counting and do not play; the note before it stops.

## Count
id: zaehlzeit
lesson: zaehlen
A beat in the bar, counted 1 2 3 4; the half beats in between are called "and".

## Eighth note
id: achtel
lesson: achtel
A note that lasts half a beat. Eight fill a 4/4 bar, counted 1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and.

## Bass drum
id: bassdrum
lesson: schlagzeug
The drum kit's low drum, played with the foot, often on 1 and 3. The bass likes to play together with it.

## Snare
id: snare
lesson: schlagzeug
The drum kit's bright, cracking drum, mostly on 2 and 4: the backbeat.

## Tablature
id: tab
for: strings
lesson: tab
Notation for string instruments: lines are strings (the lowest at the bottom), numbers say where you press; 0 is the open string.

## Semitone
id: halbton
lesson: halbton
The smallest step between two notes. Twelve semitones make an octave.

## Whole tone
id: ganzton
lesson: halbton
Two semitones, for example from C to D.

## Accidental
id: vorzeichen
lesson: halbton
♯ (sharp) raises a note by a semitone, ♭ (flat) lowers it by a semitone.

## Root
id: grundton
lesson: grundton-quinte
The note a chord or scale is named after: A in A major. Bass lines usually start on it.

## Octave
id: oktave
lesson: oktave
Twelve semitones above a note: the same note, higher. Both sound almost like one.

## Fifth
id: quinte
lesson: grundton-quinte
Seven semitones above the root. Sounds strong and open with it; root and fifth are the base of many bass lines.

## String change
id: saitenwechsel
for: e-bass
lesson: saitenwechsel
Moving from one string to the next without breaking the alternating stroke. The thumb moves along and mutes the lower strings.

## Muting
id: daempfen
for: e-bass
lesson: daempfen
Touching strings that should not sound lightly so they stay silent. Both hands help.

## Fingering
id: fingersatz
for: e-bass
lesson: fingerfolgen
Which finger of the fretting hand plays which note: 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 little finger.

## Interval
id: intervall
lesson: intervall
The distance between two notes, counted in semitones: third, fourth, fifth, seventh …

## Third
id: terz
lesson: terzen
Three (minor third) or four semitones (major third) above the root. It decides whether a chord sounds minor or major.

## Fourth
id: quarte
lesson: quarte-quinte
Five semitones above the root.

## Sixth
id: sexte
lesson: septime
Eight (minor sixth) or nine semitones (major sixth) above the root.

## Seventh
id: septime
lesson: septime
Ten (minor seventh) or eleven semitones (major seventh) above the root. The minor seventh sounds like the blues.

## Scale
id: tonleiter
lesson: dur-tonleiter
A row of notes from the root to the octave, following a fixed pattern of whole and half steps.

## Major
id: dur
lesson: dur-tonleiter
Tonality with a major third. Sounds bright; the major scale follows the pattern whole, whole, half, whole, whole, whole, half.

## Key
id: tonart
lesson: tonarten
The scale a song takes its notes from, named after its root: G major, A minor.

## Position
id: lage
for: e-bass
lesson: dur-lagen
A fixed place of the fretting hand on the neck; each finger frets one fret.

## Chord
id: akkord
lesson: dreiklang
Several notes sounding together. The bass mostly plays its root or its notes one after another.

## Triad
id: dreiklang
lesson: dreiklang
Chord of root, third and fifth. With a major third it is major, with a minor third minor.

## Arpeggio
id: arpeggio
lesson: arpeggio
The notes of a chord played one after another instead of together.

## Seventh chord
id: septakkord
lesson: septakkord
Triad with a seventh: maj7 (major seventh), 7 (major with minor seventh), m7 (minor with minor seventh).

## Degree
id: stufe
lesson: stufen
A chord's place in the key, in Roman numerals: I is the root, IV and V its most important neighbours.

## Chord symbol
id: akkordsymbol
lesson: akkordnamen
Shorthand for a chord: root and addition, such as Am7. After a slash comes the bass note.

## Minor
id: moll
lesson: moll-tonleiter
Tonality with a minor third. Sounds darker than major; the minor scale follows the pattern whole, half, whole, whole, half, whole, whole.

## Pentatonic
id: pentatonik
lesson: pentatonik
Scale of five notes without half steps. Minor pentatonic: 1, b3, 4, 5, b7.

## Chord progression
id: akkordfolge
lesson: akkordfolgen
The row of chords in a song, often written in degrees: I-V-vi-IV.

## Syncopation
id: synkope
lesson: synkope
An accented note between two beats, often held across the next beat. Gives the groove a push forward.

## Sixteenth note
id: sechzehntel
lesson: sechzehntel
A quarter of a beat. Counted "1 e and a".

## Ghost note
id: ghost-note
for: strings
lesson: ghost-notes
A very quiet, short note between accented notes, felt more than heard.

## Dead note
id: dead-note
for: strings
lesson: dead-notes
A percussive click without pitch: the fretting hand rests lightly on the string. Written x in tab.

## Bass clef
id: bassschluessel
lesson: bassschluessel
Clef for low parts; its two dots surround the F line.

## Staff lines
id: notenlinien
lesson: bassschluessel
The five lines that notes sit on and between. Higher notes sound higher.

## Stem
id: notenhals
lesson: rhythmus-lesen
The vertical line at the note head. With flags or beams it marks shorter note values.

## Key signature
id: generalvorzeichen
lesson: vorzeichen-lesen
Sharps or flats right after the clef; they hold for the whole piece and show the key.

## Natural sign
id: aufloesungszeichen
lesson: vorzeichen-lesen
♮ cancels a sharp or flat until the end of the bar.

## Time signature
id: taktart
lesson: taktarten
How many beats a bar has and which note value counts as one: 4/4, 3/4, 6/8.

## Triplet
id: triole
lesson: triole
Three equal notes in the time of two. Counted "1 trip let".

## Shuffle
id: shuffle
lesson: shuffle
Eighths long, short instead of equal; sounds like a triplet without its middle.
