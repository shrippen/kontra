## Open string
id: leersaite
for: strings
lesson: bass-teile
A string you pluck without pressing it down. Tabs write it as 0.

## Fret
id: bund
for: e-bass
lesson: greifen
The metal strips across the neck. From fret to fret the note goes up a semitone.
::: variant for=fretted
You press right behind it.
:::
::: variant for=fretless
Your bass has none; the markers show where they would be, and that is where you press.
:::

## Tuning
id: stimmen
for: strings
lesson: stimmen
Setting the strings so that each open string hits its note exactly. Before every practice, with the tuner.

## Alternate plucking
id: wechselschlag
for: e-bass
lesson: anschlag
Index and middle finger of the plucking hand take turns. The attack stays even, also when it gets faster.

## Bar
id: takt
lesson: viertel
A group of beats, usually four (1 2 3 4). The time signature says how many beats of which length: 4/4 means four
quarter notes.

## Quarter note
id: viertel
lesson: viertel
A note lasting one beat. Four quarter notes fill a 4/4 bar.

## BPM
id: bpm
lesson: viertel
Beats per minute: the tempo. 60 BPM is one beat per second, 120 BPM two.

## Metronome
id: metronom
lesson: viertel
Gives the beats as an even click. Kontra has one in every mode, and drums in the grooves.

## Note value
id: notenwert
lesson: notenwerte
How long a note or rest lasts, in beats: whole (4), half (2), quarter (1), eighth (½).

## Rest
id: pause
lesson: notenwerte
A note value without a note. You keep counting and do not play; the note before it stops.

## Count
id: zaehlzeit
lesson: zaehlen
A beat in the bar, counted 1 2 3 4; the half beats in between are called "and".

## Eighth note
id: achtel
lesson: achtel
A note that lasts half a beat. Eight fill a 4/4 bar, counted 1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and.

## Bass drum
id: bassdrum
lesson: schlagzeug
The drum kit's low drum, played with the foot, often on 1 and 3. The bass likes to play together with it.

## Snare
id: snare
lesson: schlagzeug
The drum kit's bright, cracking drum, mostly on 2 and 4: the backbeat.

## Tablature
id: tab
for: strings
lesson: tab
Notation for string instruments: lines are strings (the lowest at the bottom), numbers say where you press; 0 is the open string.

## Semitone
id: halbton
lesson: halbton
The smallest step between two notes. Twelve semitones make an octave.

## Whole tone
id: ganzton
lesson: halbton
Two semitones, for example from C to D.

## Accidental
id: vorzeichen
lesson: halbton
♯ (sharp) raises a note by a semitone, ♭ (flat) lowers it by a semitone.

## Root
id: grundton
lesson: grundton-quinte
The note a chord or scale is named after: A in A major. Bass lines usually start on it.

## Octave
id: oktave
lesson: oktave
Twelve semitones above a note: the same note, higher. Both sound almost like one.

## Fifth
id: quinte
lesson: grundton-quinte
Seven semitones above the root. Sounds strong and open with it; root and fifth are the base of many bass lines.

## String change
id: saitenwechsel
for: e-bass
lesson: saitenwechsel
Moving from one string to the next without breaking the alternating stroke. The thumb moves along and mutes the lower strings.

## Muting
id: daempfen
for: e-bass
lesson: daempfen
Touching strings that should not sound lightly so they stay silent. Both hands help.

## Fingering
id: fingersatz
for: e-bass
lesson: fingerfolgen
Which finger of the fretting hand plays which note: 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 little finger.

## Interval
id: intervall
lesson: intervall
The distance between two notes, counted in semitones: third, fourth, fifth, seventh …

## Third
id: terz
lesson: terzen
Three (minor third) or four semitones (major third) above the root. It decides whether a chord sounds minor or major.

## Fourth
id: quarte
lesson: quarte-quinte
Five semitones above the root.

## Sixth
id: sexte
lesson: septime
Eight (minor sixth) or nine semitones (major sixth) above the root.

## Seventh
id: septime
lesson: septime
Ten (minor seventh) or eleven semitones (major seventh) above the root. The minor seventh sounds like the blues.
