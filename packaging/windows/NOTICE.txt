  libraries: the DLLs in the bin folder and bin\qml\org\kde. Sources:

Kontra links these libraries dynamically: they are separate DLL files.
You may replace them with your own build of the same libraries; Kontra
needs no change for that. Kontra's own source code is at
https://github.com/shrippen/Kontra

All texts are in the folder share\licenses\Kontra of this package.
The licences of the Rust libraries inside kontra.exe are listed in
Kontra under Settings > About.
